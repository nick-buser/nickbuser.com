## The problem

Answering a cold request with a 503 punished callers. A well-behaved client
waited out a stale hint, and a client that doesn't retry 503s saw an error.

## The change

The gateway holds a cold request for up to 60 seconds, checking the model's
health every second with a bounded timeout on each check, then forwards it
normally, streaming included. Past the hold, `Retry-After` is the expected time
remaining, with a five-second floor. Sixty seconds is under the tightest known
client timeout, a 120-second chat call.

## The result

A client that obeys `Retry-After` went from 150.3 seconds to 41.4, with zero
503s. It also beat the five-second poller by about four seconds. CI caught two
defects before release: a request body that couldn't be replayed after the hold,
and a test that reused a closed HTTP client.

## The decision

Adopted.
