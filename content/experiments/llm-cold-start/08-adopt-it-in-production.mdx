## Problem

Two configurations passed the adoption rule: graphs on, about 56 seconds to a
first answer, and graphs off, about 45.

## Change

Production got the persistent driver cache, the pinned KV cache, and no CUDA
graphs. Running without graphs won on wake to first answer, at a 0.8% cost to
batch-1 decode on the twin, and the goal was the shortest cold start.

## Result

On the real pod, time to Ready went from 145 seconds to 40, with the first
256-token answer in 5.2. The first start after the change takes 81 seconds,
once, while it fills the cache.

## Decision

Shipped. Verifying it turned up the next problem.
