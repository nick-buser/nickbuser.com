## The problem

Every number so far stopped at the model. Callers go through the gateway, which
answered a cold request with a 503 and a `Retry-After` header.

## The change

An end-to-end mode for the harness. It scales the model to zero, sends a
streaming request through the gateway, follows the 503s in one of two client
styles, and times the first token.

## The result

The model was Ready at 41 seconds. A client that obeyed `Retry-After: 150`, a
value sized for the old start, got its first token at 150.3 seconds. A client
retrying every five seconds got it at 45.6. A warm request took 0.06 seconds,
and runs agreed within a few hundredths of a second.

## The decision

The deciding number became a real client's wait. The stale retry hint was
costing a well-behaved caller about 109 seconds.
