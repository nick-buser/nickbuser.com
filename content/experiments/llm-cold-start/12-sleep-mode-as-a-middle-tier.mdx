## The problem

Between awake and off, vLLM's sleep mode might give a fast wake without holding
the GPU.

## The change

Both sleep levels on a twin, measuring GPU memory, host memory, the time back to
serving, and whether answers matched before and after.

<Figure caption="Three views of the trade: how fast it comes back, what it frees on the card, and what it parks on the host.">
  <ColdStartBars set="cs6" />
</Figure>

## The result

Both levels freed about 11.8 GB of the card and answered identically after
waking. Level 1 came back in 0.8 seconds but parked 14.1 GiB in host memory and
still held it after waking, on a host that has hung under memory pressure
before. Level 2 parked nothing and served again in 2.0 seconds with a warm page
cache, or 16.9 with a cold one.

In both cases the sleeping pod kept its Kueue quota. A training job got the card
in four seconds only by preempting it, the same as from an awake model.

## The decision

Not adopted as it stands. Level 2 becomes a third state, beside awake and off,
in the analysis of how long to keep the model up.
