## The problem

Step 11 showed the engine process importing every library a second time,
because it was spawned.

## The change

`VLLM_WORKER_MULTIPROC_METHOD=fork`. At about 14% of the start it fell short of
the 20% rule, so adopting it was a judgment call, and I made it.

</Figure>

## The result

On the real pod, the time before the engine starts fell from 22 seconds to
17–18, and Ready from 40 to 35–36. A cold caller's first token came at 36.54,
36.50 and 41.48 seconds, with no 503s. The third run fell on the readiness
probe's next five-second step.

<Figure caption="The whole series in one picture: where a start's seconds went at the baseline, and where they go now.">
  <StartupAnatomy />
</Figure>

## The decision

Adopted. Verifying it turned up one more problem. An automated client, probably
an uptime check, was requesting the gateway's root and model list about every 30
minutes, and each request woke the 7B. With a 60-minute idle window, that can
keep the model up indefinitely. Only inference requests should wake it, and that
change is next.
