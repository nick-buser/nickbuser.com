## Problem

The decode benchmark timed the first requests after start, so it measured
warm-up along with decoding.

## Change

One timed warm-up request before the decode benchmark, reported on its own.

## Result

Steady decode was within 2% in every arm. The graph-less arms paid about 40
seconds on their first request instead of at startup. For a caller, wake to
first full answer went from about 135 seconds to about 125 at best: 7%, not
38%.

<Figure caption="The same arms, three ways. Steady decode is within 2% everywhere.">

</Figure>

## Decision

From here on, a change was judged on wake to first answer, not on time to Ready.
