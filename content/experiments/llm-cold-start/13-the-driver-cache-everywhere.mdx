## The problem

Every other GPU pod on the card had the same temporary driver cache, and each
paid its own compile on every start.

## The change

The setting moved into the shared Helm chart template that every vLLM service
uses.

## The result

It now covers the embedding server and the on-demand 12B. The speech and vision
services were measured first. Their driver caches were 112 KB or less, so they
were left alone.

## The decision

Adopted. Whether the 12B gets the same gain shows on its next wakes.
