## Problem

Found while verifying the production change: the LoRA adapter loaded 21 seconds
after the model was Ready. Its loader backed off up to 30 seconds between health
checks, which was invisible against a 140-second start.

## Change

A fixed two-second health check.

## Result

The adapter now loads about a second before the pod reports Ready. The first
version of the new unit test also passed against the old code. That was caught
and fixed before merge.

## Decision

Fixed. A request for the adapter no longer waits on a back-off timer after the
model is up.
