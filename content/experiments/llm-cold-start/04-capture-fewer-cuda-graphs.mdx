## The problem

Capturing CUDA graphs still took 52 seconds of a 130-second start.

## The change

Four arms: fewer capture sizes, piecewise graphs only, piecewise with fewer
sizes, and no graphs at all with `--enforce-eager`.

<Figure caption="Switch to the decode view: the faster starts came with a decode loss far too large for the change.">
  <ColdStartBars set="cs2" />
</Figure>

## The result

Piecewise graphs started in 90 seconds, piecewise with fewer sizes in 80, and no
graphs in 81: 36–43% shorter than the baseline. Batch-1 decode in every
graph-less arm appeared to fall from 52 to 14 tokens per second.

## The decision

A 73% decode loss didn't fit a change this size, so it was treated as a
measurement error until shown otherwise.
